# Business Content Framework Evaluation Contract

## Source Contribution

The Business Content Framework contributes business-goal, audience, evidence, action, and diagnostic discipline. It does not contribute SEO tool selection or page-writing procedures to this skill.

Confidence: `A1` for the source behavior and `B2` for its adaptation to SEO analysis.

## Evaluation Criteria

An SEO recommendation is incomplete when it assumes a business purpose without identifying the outcome, intended customer action, functional buyer profile, and explicit disqualifications relevant to the page.

Audience evaluation should identify the reader's prior knowledge, journey stage, carried intent, and consumption context when those fields affect page depth, proof, format, or action.

Important claims require concrete attributable evidence. The SEO agent must record missing proof as a gap and must not ask a downstream writer to invent it.

Underperformance diagnosis must test the user's hypothesis against the available signals. The source fixture demonstrates that above-baseline reach can contradict a weak-opening hypothesis and that the correct response is to locate the earliest failed condition before changing multiple layers.

## SEO Projection

Project these principles into the SEO skill:

- Confirm the page's business job and primary user action when evaluating conversion-related content.
- Distinguish a missing business input from an SEO observation.
- Test causal hypotheses against available ranking, traffic, engagement, content, or technical evidence.
- Identify the earliest supported failure layer.
- Recommend the smallest intervention that addresses that layer.
- Preserve evidence-bearing material and valid strategic decisions.

## Handoff Fields

When relevant and supported, a downstream implementation handoff should include:

- Business outcome and primary user action.
- Functional audience description and journey state.
- Disqualified audiences or actions.
- Required evidence or proof and unresolved proof gaps.
- One primary next action.
- Acceptance criteria tied to the diagnosed failure.

Fields that require owner knowledge rather than SEO evidence must be marked `required-user-input` instead of inferred as fact.

## Excluded Material

Do not project the nine-layer production workflow, theme or angle generation, piece construction, story structures, emotional targets, medium selection, channel selection, publication packages, or copy examples.
