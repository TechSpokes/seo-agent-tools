# Choose evidence for the question

Confirm what the business counts as a meaningful outcome and identify the site and property. Record dates, property timezone, acquisition or attribution scope, filters, dimensions and metric definitions. Establish the web stream only if it affects the question. For configuration, inspect current settings and key-event definitions. If historical validity matters, also inspect observed reporting over the requested dates. Today's setting alone cannot reconstruct what a past report meant.

For landing-page outcomes, align sessions, engagement and validated key events to the same landing-page and organic scope. For progression, obtain ordered-user funnel evidence with compatible definitions. For current activity, a realtime observation has only its short window. A file name gives no evidence until the host inspects its readable fields. Chargeable requests require current authorization and a bounded quote.

Consult the [evidence plan](evidence-plan.json) for exact stage IDs, evidence bounds and completion criteria.
