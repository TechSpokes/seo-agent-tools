# Content Question Coverage Review

Assess one identified document or page capture against a fixed audience question list. Preserve question text and order, cite the material for each judgment and recommend a bounded revision for actual gaps. A supplied readable artifact and question list need no paid acquisition. Site inventory, demand estimates and publishing fall outside this review.

## Use the selected stages

1. [Inspect the document and assign statuses](references/inspection-and-status.md) before collecting the observations for that decision.
2. [Recommend revisions and settle completion](references/revisions-and-decisions.md) when the first stage supplies the required evidence. Follow a documented earlier stop when its conditions apply.

The [evidence plan](references/evidence-plan.json) gives the exact inputs, ordered steps, evidence bounds, conditions and stop rules. Check applicable obligations before dependent work. Keep this catalog and recipe revision when following references. An explicit full-package read is available for inspection.
