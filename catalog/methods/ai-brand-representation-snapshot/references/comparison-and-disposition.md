# Compare eligible observations

Compare report content only where at least two channels returned reports, with evidence from both eligible rows for each claim. Recognition-status comparison may include valid not-recognized outcomes. Keep controlled-panel comparisons separate from generated competitors and supplied products separate from generated associations. Report a finite native score, including zero, only in its channel context. Generated source descriptors have no independent citation verification.

Return exactly one row for each channel. Three reports permit proceed. Otherwise at least two valid reports or not-recognized outcomes permit conditional. Fewer than two valid outcomes require defer, except unresolved or invalid framing before work requires reject. Completion requires three valid observations; any post-start failed channel leaves the snapshot incomplete. Reconcile costs from actual quotes and charges. Claims about historical change, unprompted presence or business effect require different evidence.
