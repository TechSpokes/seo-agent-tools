# Compare observed referring-domain sets

Accept up to three confirmed or suitably discovered competitors. Record source scope, dates and limits for the observed target and competitor domain sets. Check equivalent filters and periods before comparing them. A candidate appears in an observed competitor set and is unobserved in the comparable target subset. Preserve direct or contradictory target-link evidence and reject unsupported domain-wide absence claims.

Screen for relevance to the target's actual market and assets. Use authority metrics only when they can distinguish plausible finalists, recording provenance and freshness. Repeated domains or target links already established by the evidence can remove candidates before any page reads. If screening leaves none, close with an empty scoped set and explain the decisive observation.

Consult the [evidence plan](evidence-plan.json) for exact stage IDs, evidence bounds and completion criteria.
