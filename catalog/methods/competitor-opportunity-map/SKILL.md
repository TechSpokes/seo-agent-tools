# Competitor Opportunity Map

Find search opportunities for one domain by comparing competitors that genuinely serve its audience. Establish the market and target site role first. Every retained cluster needs current intent evidence, target coverage and a feasible reason the target can serve the need.

## Use the selected stages

1. [Confirm competitors and collect candidates](references/competitor-selection.md) before collecting the observations for that decision.
2. [Validate clusters and target ownership](references/opportunity-validation.md) when the first stage supplies the required evidence. Follow a documented earlier stop when its conditions apply.

The [evidence plan](references/evidence-plan.json) gives the exact inputs, ordered steps, evidence bounds, conditions and stop rules. Check applicable obligations before dependent work. Keep this catalog and recipe revision when following references. An explicit full-package read is available for inspection.

## Deliver the result

- [seo-opportunity-set/v1](contracts/seo-opportunity-set.v1.schema.json) is the primary deliverable for complete and incomplete results.

Use the exact contract and its local dependencies before dependent work, reusing versions already loaded from this publication. A narrower or provisional conclusion still uses the contract's completion states. Repair formatting against retained observations. The [worked case](examples/worked-case.md) illustrates the decision.
