# Content Production Workflow

Coordinate one content artifact from its business objective to the requested milestone. Identify the owner, audience, approved scope and required evidence. Drafting, factual sign-off and publication each require a separately authorized actor. A supported editorial handoff can proceed while publication remains pending.

## Use the selected stages

1. [Prepare and inspect the canonical document](references/brief-and-document.md) before collecting the observations for that decision.
2. [Prepare the handoff and verify the published page](references/handoff-and-verification.md) when the first stage supplies the required evidence. Follow a documented earlier stop when its conditions apply.

The [evidence plan](references/evidence-plan.json) gives the exact inputs, ordered steps, evidence bounds, conditions and stop rules. Check applicable obligations before dependent work. Keep this catalog and recipe revision when following references. An explicit full-package read is available for inspection.

## Deliver the result

- [seo-implementation-handoff/v1](contracts/seo-implementation-handoff.v1.schema.json) is the primary deliverable for complete and incomplete results.
- [content-question-review/v1](contracts/content-question-review.v1.schema.json) applies under the following condition. Emit when the approved content context contains buyer or people-also-ask questions.

Use the exact contract and its local dependencies before dependent work, reusing versions already loaded from this publication. A narrower or provisional conclusion still uses the contract's completion states. Repair formatting against retained observations.
