# Prepare the handoff and verify the published page

Transfer the artifact identity, approval state, target URL, evidence-backed requirements, known limitations and preservation rules. State the owner, remaining claims to verify, decision needed and acceptance checks. Never place finished copy inside the handoff. A handoff containing completed preparation and outstanding publication steps is useful. Mark an end-to-end request incomplete when required publication or verification is missing.

Obtain approval for the exact revision and destination before routing publication. Observing a draft or receiving publication instructions gives no CMS authority. Verify public delivery at the intended URL, including the required content, metadata and technical signals. Use a sufficiently complete authorized live capture of the named page where the method permits it. Record a review trigger and owner for time-limited content. Keep judgment about eventual retention or redirection with that owner.

Consult the [evidence plan](evidence-plan.json) for exact stage IDs, evidence bounds and completion criteria.
