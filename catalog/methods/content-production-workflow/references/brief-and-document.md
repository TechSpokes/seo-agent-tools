# Prepare and inspect the canonical document

Settle the audience need, page role, goal, conversion path, market and editorial constraints before research. Use one bounded research burst only if it can change the outline, questions or site routing. Record the approved outline and the original wording and order of buyer questions. A separate content capability prepares one canonical document. Retain its identity, body, format, intended SEO title and meta description. Use known explicit metadata, recognized opening frontmatter or the full HTML head. Missing title or description blocks required document analysis.

Check the canonical document against the approved scope and material factual claims. Use automated scores as inputs to inspect. Verify facts against primary, first-party or supplied evidence independently. For an approved buyer question, cite both its source and the passage or inspected absence, preserve order, and recommend revisions for partial and unanswered questions. Put new questions in the supplemental list. Resolve retrievable facts before asking an owner for a material decision.

Consult the [evidence plan](evidence-plan.json) for exact stage IDs, evidence bounds and completion criteria.
