# Compare pages and prepare the brief

After the technical precheck, inspect current results for up to three representative queries, plus the target and up to three representative pages. A complete authorized capture may support comparison when it includes the relevant page material and provenance. Partial extracted text can support a statement about the text seen. It cannot establish what the complete or rendered page omits. Check intent, role, overlap, evidence quality and unexplained differences before prescribing content.

Use observed evidence to test competing content, technical, strategic and operational explanations. Trace a score to its underlying observations and resolve or disclose material conflict. If required result evidence fails after execution begins, preserve valid target observations and identify the missing layer, prohibited recommendations, recovery and condition for resuming. Defer the diagnostic and omit the handoff. A completed diagnostic supports a handoff only when the intervention is ready, with preservation requirements and an observable verification.

Consult the [evidence plan](evidence-plan.json) for exact stage IDs, evidence bounds and completion criteria.
