# Confirm competitors and collect candidates

Record the target domain, audience, business scope, market and constraints. Use supplied competitors when defensible or bounded discovery when needed. Distinguish search overlap from business relevance. Explain material rejections and keep the confirmed set within the method's bounds. A competitor's high ranking or a generated query can suggest a candidate but cannot establish target fit.

Collect only query evidence that can affect the decision. Preserve the source, market, timing, query grouping and limitations of any metrics. Stop or narrow the affected branch before unauthorized acquisition. Use current evidence for bounded finalists and reserve page inspection for questions the result evidence cannot settle.

Consult the [evidence plan](evidence-plan.json) for exact stage IDs, evidence bounds and completion criteria.
