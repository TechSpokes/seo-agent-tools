# Backlink Gap Prospecting

Choose referring domains worth investigating for one target and a bounded group of relevant competitors. Confirm the market, target assets, outreach constraints and comparable link evidence. Source-page inspection becomes necessary only for finalists that survive screening.

## Use the selected stages

1. [Compare observed referring-domain sets](references/sets-and-screening.md) before collecting the observations for that decision.
2. [Qualify the surviving prospects](references/finalist-qualification.md) when the first stage supplies the required evidence. Follow a documented earlier stop when its conditions apply.

The [evidence plan](references/evidence-plan.json) gives the exact inputs, ordered steps, evidence bounds, conditions and stop rules. Check applicable obligations before dependent work. Keep this catalog and recipe revision when following references. An explicit full-package read is available for inspection.
