# Inspect the document and assign statuses

Record the document revision or capture identity, time, format and readable extent. Locate the list that established the questions and its order. Use the supplied audience or purpose where it changes what constitutes an adequate answer. For every supplied position, reproduce its wording exactly. Keep repeated positions when the source repeats one. Link the governing question and the relevant document passage or checked extent within each row. Carry question-list and document evidence in separate readable source records.

Use answered for a passage that answers the actual question. Use partially_answered when a passage addresses part and leaves a specific material part missing. Use not_answered when inspection of sufficient relevant content establishes the gap. Use unable_to_determine when missing, illegible or conflicting material prevents assessment. Cite the limitation itself and the usable context. Record supplemental questions separately, even when they would improve the document. An isolated snippet cannot establish page-wide absence.

Consult the [evidence plan](evidence-plan.json) for exact stage IDs, evidence bounds and completion criteria.
